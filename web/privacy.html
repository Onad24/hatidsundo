Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create or modify your account, request on-demand services, contact customer support, or otherwise communicate with us. This information may include: name, email, phone number, profile picture, payment method, and other information you choose to provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect about you to provide, maintain, and improve our services, such as to facilitate payments, send receipts, provide products and services you request (and send related information), develop new features, provide customer support to Users and Drivers, develop safety features, authenticate users, and send product updates and administrative messages.

3. Location Information

When you use the services for transportation, we collect precise location data about the trip from the Hatid Sundo app used by the Driver. If you permit the Hatid Sundo app to access location services through the permission system used by your mobile operating system, we may also collect the precise location of your device when the app is running in the foreground or background.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share the information we collect about you with drivers to enable them to provide the services you request. For example, we share your name, photo (if you provide one), and pickup and/or drop-off locations with drivers.

5. Data Deletion Requests

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated personal data from our systems, please send an email to hatidsundo.tanauan@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will process your request and permanently remove your data in accordance with applicable laws.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hatidsundo.tanauan@gmail.com.
